# ADR-002: Ship Cwd And Branch Only

## Status

Accepted

## Date

2026-08-20

## Context

Issue 6 proposes three identity segments: `cwd`, `branch`, and optional `path`. Later candidates include dirty-tree, ahead/behind, and hostname. Acceptance only requires `cwd` and `branch`. Extra segments expand settings, picker, completions, and paint-order surface area.

## Decision

This slice ships only `cwd` and `branch`. Leave `path`, dirty, ahead/behind, and hostname out of settings, help, picker, and `StatuslineItems`.

## Alternatives Considered

### Alternative 1: cwd + branch + path

- **Description**: Also persist and render a compact path segment after cwd.
- **Pros**: Disambiguates duplicate folder names in one change.
- **Cons**: Needs path compaction rules and another width-sensitive segment.
- **Why rejected**: Acceptance does not require it, and cwd plus branch already identify most workspaces.

### Alternative 2: Include later markers now

- **Description**: Add dirty, ahead/behind, and hostname in the same slice.
- **Pros**: Completes the identity bar earlier.
- **Cons**: Dirty and ahead/behind need `git status` or equivalent, which issue 6 forbids on the paint path.
- **Why rejected**: Those markers are not cheap enough for this footer.

## Consequences

### Positive

- Keeps the toggle and persist path a mechanical extension of sandbox/context/session.
- Leaves room for `path` later without rewriting identity ownership.

### Negative

- Duplicate folder names stay ambiguous until a later `path` segment.

### Risks

- Users may immediately ask for `path`. Mitigation: keep `StatuslineItems` and append order easy to extend.

## Implementation Notes

Append order remains: core, session, cwd, branch, sandbox, context.
